Leftover Chicken Bowl

Take that left over chicken or frozen precooked chicken and dress it up!

Cook time: 15 mins

Prep time: 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Leftover/Precooked meat: 4 oz
  • Frozen Rice: 1 cup
  • chopped bellpepper : 1/2 cup
  • chopped onion : 1/2 cup
  • beans (any): 1 can
  • shredded carrots: any amount
  • Lettuce: any

Instructions

  1. Step 1: If using frozen precooked meat or rice follow instructions on packaging.
  2. Step 2: Chop up meat to bite sized pieces if not already small enought
  3. Step 3: Chop a bell pepper and onion. Can you use pre chopped or frozen medley of peppers and onion (follow package instructions)
  4. Step 4: Drain and rinse beans
  5. Step 5: Assemble bowl layer rice then meat then veggies in any order.
  6. Step 6: Top with shredded cheese and any sauces you may have or try one below!
