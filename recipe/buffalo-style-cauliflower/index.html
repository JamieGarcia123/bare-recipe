Buffalo Style Cauliflower

Easy, quick hassle free vegetarian appetizer!

Cook time: 15 mins

Prep time: 10 mins

Ingredients

  • Cauliflower: 1 head (chopped)
  • Flour: 1-2 cups
  • water: about 1 cups
  • salt: 1 tsp
  • pepper: 1 tsp
  • Franks Red Hot (or any hot sauce): 1/4 cup
  • Butter (optional): 1 tbs

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Grab a deep pan that will fit 3" of cooking oil.
  2. Step 2: Fill pan with your choice of cooking oil.
  3. Step 3: Heat oil to 350f.
  4. Step 4: While heating oil chop you head of cauliflower into bite size bites.
  5. Step 5: Mix flour and spices in a bowl
  6. Step 6: Whisk in half of the water to flour
  7. Step 7: Add more water into batter until you get a pancake batter like consistency. Should stick to the cauliflower but not be too think
  8. Step 8: Add cauliflower bits to the batter and mix around to coat well
  9. Step 9: You will want to use a spoon to retrieve bits to fry up
  10. Step 10: Drop a good amount into your oil leave space so they float.
  11. Step 11: Once they float use a straining ladle to slowly mix the oil to see if the batter is crispy/golden.
  12. Step 12: Remove when ready onto a plate with a layer of paper towels to drain.
  13. Step 13: Once all the cauliflower is fried grab another bowl and add franks red hot (if original add 1tbs of butter)
  14. Step 14: Once sauce has butter melted in (if using) add the fried bits
  15. Step 15: Mix with a spoon or toss if you got the skills.
  16. Step 16: Enjoy and serve with ranch or bleu cheese.
