Baked Chicken

Have some chicken laying around? Let's fix it up!

Cook time: 25 mins

Prep time: 15 mins

Ingredients

  • chicken: 4 peices any cut
  • olive oil: 2 tbs
  • salt: 2 tsp
  • pepper: 1 tsp
  • paprika (smoked or not): 1 tbs
  • garlic powder: 1 tbs
  • salt: to taste
  • pepper: to taste
  • oregano or thyme: 2 tsp

Instructions

  1. Step 1: preheat oven to 425f
  2. Step 2: add spices of choice to oil
  3. Step 3: if its a little dry still add 1 tbs more oil should mix well and be a thick 'batter like' consitency
  4. Step 4: at this point any spices will do... try ground clove or ground cinnamon, may lemon zest or lemon juice added...
  5. Step 5: mix in chicken pieces to coat well
  6. Step 6: optionally you can let this set in the bowl for 30 minutes in the fridge or while your oven preheats.
  7. Step 7: place chicken pieces in a large enough baking dish ("9x13" usually)
  8. Step 8: bake for 25 minutes
